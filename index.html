Decision-focused learning and policy adaptation

I study how learned human and environment models affect policy choice in sequential interaction. My work combines offline and inverse reinforcement learning, action-conditioned dynamics, and explicit evaluation of the decision value of information.

Recent studies use reachability-certified policy families, matched intervention controls, and independent controller refitting to attribute gains in human–AI interaction. In CARLA, I compare direct visual action-value learning with survival-coupled objectives under conflicting front- and rear-collision risks, using matched heads and losses to separate predictive improvement from driving outcomes.

Decision Value of Human Models Under Certified Policy Families and Independent RefittingLearning Longitudinal Control Decisions for Autonomous Driving Under Conflicting Collision Risks

System identification, active sensing, and control

My work evaluates what physical observations make identifiable and how effectively learned estimators and planners use that evidence. A MuJoCo/MJX and JAX protocol combines successful-action sets, finite-pool decision-conflict certificates, estimator/planner interventions, and locked waveform selection under matched sensing and actuation budgets.

A complementary manipulation study isolates the force-measurement interface of a fixed controller across Bullet, PhysX, and MuJoCo. It tests how physical-time averaging, history operators, and guard-check timing alter execution when dynamics and ordinary feedback remain fixed.

Evaluating Physical Probes for Task Decisions under Hidden DynamicsSame Threshold, Different Guard: Temporal Force Measurement in Simulated Manipulation

Representation analysis and reasoning-model audits

I probe spatial information in frozen visual reasoning representations using answer-preserving scene edits, pre-answer references, relation readouts, and coordinate probes. Answer/confidence, prompt-state, and visual-encoder controls delimit what high probe accuracy establishes about latent computation.

My reasoning-model audit crosses four weight precisions with three thinking-token budgets over 9,840 generations. It combines paired capability and safety batteries, independent LLM assessors, blinded human review, and statistical analysis to distinguish model behavior from assessor sensitivity under deployment constraints.
